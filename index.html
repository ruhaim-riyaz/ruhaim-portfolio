Hi, I’m Ruhaim Riyaz, a young creative professional based in Sri Lanka. My creative journey is fueled by a passion for visual storytelling, bridging the gap between aesthetics and meaning. Through Visuals by Ruhaim, my creative identity, I specialize in both photography and graphic design.

Whether I am capturing the intricate details of a cultural event or designing comprehensive branding systems and leadership campaigns, my goal remains the same: to tell stories through powerful visuals that make an impact. My leadership roles have also shaped my ability to direct creative projects and guide teams to success.
